Introducing Gannopy Urena

Gannopy Urena (they/them) is a student journalist at the Craig Newmark School of Journalism. Their reporting interests are labor, transporation, and arts and culture. They spend a lot of time thinking about the value of music and clothing, as well as getting the latest scoop at their local gay bar.


Here are my top 3 favorite stories:

  • Ssential Workers - Lux Magazine

    • A deep dive into the popular online fashion retailer and how their uncool labor practices led to their downfall. I think it's always fascinating how the places that seem like the coolest places to work always end up being the most inhumane.

  • The Race to Upcycle Africa's Fast Fashion Dumping Ground - Time Magazine

    • A look into how people living in Ghana are tacking the thousands of pounds of textile waste dumped on them from western countries. Upcycling and reusing old clothing is an action we all need to start incorporating into our lives to combat the climate crisis.

  • Japanese Scientists Find Method to Support Sustainable Polyster Recycling - Women's Wear Daily

    • A huge milestone has been reached in figuring out how to recycle polysester. These scientists in Japan haven't tested this method widely yet, but I'm excited to see what the new possibilites for textile recycling will be.
